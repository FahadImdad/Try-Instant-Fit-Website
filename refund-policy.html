Refund Policy

Effective: 13 August 2026

Try-on credit refunds

If a completed AI try-on or garment-processing result is materially defective because of the Try Instant Fit service, a customer or vendor may submit a report. Approved reports return the applicable credit to the vendor account. The dashboard Refunds tab records the credit and refund time.

Review timing

We aim to review complete reports within 24 hours, but complex or incomplete cases may take longer. Submitting a report does not guarantee approval.

Not normally refundable

Credits are not normally refunded for change of mind, unsuitable source photos, unsupported garments, inaccurate vendor product information, customer device/connectivity problems, passcode misuse, or results used despite an obvious error. We do not refund a vendor’s garment sale; product returns and custom-size orders are handled by the vendor under its own policy and applicable consumer law.

Duplicate and abusive claims

A try-on can receive at most one credit refund. Fraudulent, automated or repeated abusive claims may be denied and access may be restricted.

Payment/top-up refunds

Unused top-ups may be considered individually where required by law or agreed in writing. Used credits and incurred third-party processing costs are generally non-refundable, subject to mandatory consumer rights.

How to request review

Use “Something wrong with this result?” after a try-on or contact support@tryinstantfit.com with the relevant product, date and issue. Do not email sensitive photos unless support specifically requests a secure method.
