Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 August 2026

This policy is an operational draft pending review by qualified privacy counsel.

Information we process

We process vendor account and contact details, brand logos, product images and metadata, QR/passcode activity, payment/top-up records, support communications, technical logs, and customer photos submitted for virtual try-on. Issue reports may include the input photo, result, screenshot and message when needed for review.

How we use information

We use information to provide AI try-ons, operate catalogues and dashboards, enforce usage, prevent abuse, process support and refunds, maintain security, comply with law and improve service quality. We do not authorise vendors to use customer photos for unrelated advertising or identification.

AI and service providers

Submitted content may be processed by contracted infrastructure, storage and AI providers necessary to deliver the service, including cloud hosting and model providers. Providers receive only data needed for their function and are subject to applicable contractual controls.

Retention

Try-on photos and results are retained only as operationally necessary. A customer-facing result may not be persistently saved unless required for delivery, security or an issue report. Account, billing, consent, audit and refund records may be retained longer for legal and operational needs. Backups expire on their normal cycle.

Sharing

We share data with service providers, the relevant vendor where necessary to fulfil the requested experience, professional advisers, or authorities when legally required. We do not sell personal information.

Security

We use access controls and reasonable technical safeguards, but no online system is risk-free. Users must protect account links, passcodes and devices.

Your choices and requests

You may request access, correction or deletion where applicable, object to certain processing, or withdraw optional consent. Some records must be retained for fraud, accounting, legal claims or security. Contact privacy@tryinstantfit.com.

Children and sensitive use

The service is not intended for independent use by children. A lawful parent or guardian must authorise any child image. Do not submit intimate images or use the service for biometric identification.

International processing and changes

Cloud providers may process information outside your country with appropriate safeguards. We will update the effective date when this policy changes materially.
